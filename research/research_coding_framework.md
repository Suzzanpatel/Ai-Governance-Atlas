
Coding Framework

Each relevant provision or paragraph will be examined using the following dimensions.

Dimension - What to examine 
Transparency - Disclosure and information requirements 
Accountability - Responsibility and oversight 
Risk Assessment - Identification and management of AI risks 
Safety and Testing - Evaluation, robustness and safety measures 
Enforcement - Monitoring, penalties and compliance mechanisms 

## Obligation Classification

- Mandatory: Legally required obligation.
- Recommended: Official recommendation or guidance.
- Voluntary: Optional commitment.
- Institutional: Responsibility assigned to a regulator or public institution.
- Not identified: No relevant provision found in the examined material.

## Validation

Automated classifications will be manually reviewed against the original source documents.

The legal status and context of every provision must be considered before assigning a classification.
