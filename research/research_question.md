
# AI Governance in Practice

## Research Question

To what extent do India, the European Union and the United Kingdom translate AI governance principles into specific, measurable and enforceable regulatory obligations?

## Research Objectives

1. Examine the AI governance approaches adopted by India, the EU and the UK.
2. Compare their approaches to transparency, accountability, risk assessment, safety and enforcement.
3. Identify differences between voluntary governance principles and legally binding obligations.
4. Use Natural Language Processing (NLP) to identify patterns in official AI policy and regulatory documents.
5. Evaluate the limitations of using computational methods to assess regulatory effectiveness.

## Research Scope

Jurisdictions:
- India
- European Union
- United Kingdom

International reference:
- OECD AI Principles

Governance dimensions:
1. Transparency
2. Accountability
3. Risk Assessment
4. Safety and Testing
5. Enforcement

## Proposed Methodology

- Collection of official AI governance documents.
- PDF text extraction using Python.
- Text preprocessing and keyword analysis.
- Comparative analysis of governance themes.
- Manual legal classification and validation.
- Data visualisation and interpretation.

## Expected Contribution

The project aims to develop an evidence-based comparison of how different jurisdictions translate AI governance principles into regulatory obligations and institutional mechanisms.

## Research Limitations

- Differences in legal status and document types.
- Differences in document length and publication dates.
- Limitations of automated keyword and NLP analysis.
- The need for manual legal interpretation.
