## 4. Interpretation limits

1. These results describe the selected provisions and their current coding; they are not a complete inventory of each jurisdiction's AI governance.
2. A dimension with no coded provision for a jurisdiction is a gap in this dataset's coverage, not proof that the jurisdiction has no relevant rule.
3. A higher share for a dimension does not show that the jurisdiction regulates that issue more strongly or effectively.
4. Differences may reflect the selection of instruments, provision-level sampling, classification choices, or differences in document structure.
5. The concentration index describes the distribution of this sample only. It is not a regulatory quality score.
6. Phase 12 pending corrections have not been applied automatically.
