## 3. Responsibility and applicability

The file `phase13_4_responsible_party_by_applicability.csv` cross-tabulates the coded actor labels against the recorded applicability categories. It can help identify whether provisions are directed at providers, deployers, public authorities, or other categories as represented in the dataset. The underlying wording must be checked before drawing legal conclusions.

## 4. Questions for comparative legal interpretation

1. Which actors are assigned direct duties by the selected provisions?
2. Are duties allocated to developers/providers, deployers/users, regulators, public authorities, or multiple actors?
3. Are responsibilities clearly allocated, shared, conditional, or dependent on implementing measures?
4. Does the instrument distinguish the actor who must comply from the institution that supervises or enforces compliance?
5. Are there accountability or coordination gaps apparent from the primary legal texts?

## 5. Limitations

- Responsible-party labels reproduce the dataset's coding and may combine actors with legally distinct roles.
- A coded actor label does not by itself establish the exact scope of that actor's legal duty.
- The analysis does not infer institutional capacity, independence, coordination quality, or actual compliance.
- The selected provisions are not a complete inventory of each jurisdiction's allocation of responsibility.
- Pending Phase 12 corrections have not been automatically applied.
